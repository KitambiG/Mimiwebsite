Privacy Policy

How Mimi and Insync Technologies Company Limited handle information you provide through this website and platform.

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you request a demo or contact us, such as your name, property name, email, phone number and details about your property.

When you use the Mimi platform, we process the operational data you enter, such as reservations, guests and payments, on behalf of your property.

How We Use Information

To arrange demonstrations, respond to enquiries, provide and improve the Mimi platform, and communicate with you about your account and our services.

Data Security

We apply secure authentication, role-based access, property-level data isolation and secure connections to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable measures to safeguard your data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the platform, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

Your Choices

You can contact us to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and contractual requirements.

Contact

For any privacy questions, please contact Insync Technologies Company Limited through our contact page.
