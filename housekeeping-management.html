Room Statuses

One Shared Language for Room Status

Every room shows its exact state, so nobody sells a dirty room or holds up a clean one.

CleanCleaned and ready to inspect
DirtyNeeds cleaning after departure
CleaningCurrently being serviced
InspectedChecked by a supervisor
ReadyReady to sell and assign
OccupiedGuest currently in-house
Out of OrderTemporarily unavailable
Out of ServiceRemoved from inventory
The Workflow

From Check-out to Ready — Automatically

Mimi keeps reception and housekeeping perfectly in sync, in real time.

1

Guest checks out

The room automatically becomes dirty.

2

Housekeeping receives the task

The right team member is assigned to clean.

3

Cleaning starts

Status updates to cleaning so everyone can see progress.

4

Supervisor inspection

A supervisor inspects and approves the room.

5

Room marked ready

The room is cleaned, inspected and ready to sell.

6

Reception sees availability

Availability updates instantly for the front desk.
