Common Questions

Does Mimi process payments?

Mimi records and tracks payments against reservations and accounts. Payment method availability can be configured to match how your property operates.

Can I track company and agent credit?

Yes. Company and travel-agent accounts can hold credit terms, with outstanding balances and billing history tracked in one place.
