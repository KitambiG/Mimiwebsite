FAQ

Common Questions

Can Mimi handle group and travel-agent bookings?

Yes. Mimi supports individual, walk-in, group, corporate and travel-agent reservations, each with the right rates, contacts and billing arrangements.

Does the calendar prevent double-bookings?

The reservation calendar shows live availability for every room, and Mimi flags conflicts so your team books with confidence.

Can I record deposits and balances?

Every reservation tracks the total, deposit required, deposit paid and balance due, so nothing slips through.
